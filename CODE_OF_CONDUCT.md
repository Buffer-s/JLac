# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep discussion technical and on topic.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and that "no" usually
  comes with a reason.
- Give credit for work you build on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly reopening closed decisions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock or delete contributions that break these rules, and
may block accounts that keep breaking them. Report problems to the maintainers
via [GitHub](https://github.com/bufferclick/JLac/issues).

This is a shortened version of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
concept, adapted for a small project.
